### 🏢 AI Business Strategy Copilot — *the AI Co-Founder that turns a raw idea into an investor-ready business*

**The problem:** Founders validate ideas with guesswork, research competitors across scattered tabs, and pay consultants thousands for financial models and pitch prep.

**What I built:** A 9-module AI-guided Business Journey — Interview → Idea Validation → Strategy → Competitor Intelligence → Business Model Canvas → Financial Planning → Risk Intelligence → Investor Readiness → Execution Roadmap — where each module's output feeds the next. Architected on a **dual AI provider pipeline** (Gemini primary with multi-key rotation, Groq Llama 3.3 70B automatic failover) and live business intelligence via Tavily, with a fully isolated **multi-tenant security model** so every founder's startup data stays sandboxed.

**My role:** Full-stack development and AI integration — building the FastAPI backend, the AI provider factory/failover logic, and the React 19 + TanStack frontend, as part of a 4-person team.

**Outcome:** 🏆 **Top 5 Finalist — Venture Capital Hackathon**, hosted by Malla Reddy University, presenting live to judges.
